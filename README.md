## Nightcap

Nightcap is a concept for a digital tool that helps people spend their evenings more intentionally. It was designed for my Type & Interaction 2 class at Parsons MPS Communication Design. 

It's built around a common problem: when the day winds down and decision fatigue sets in, most of us default to whatever requires the least effort, usually doomscrolling, rather than doing something we'd actually find enjoyable or meaningful. Nightcap explores how a product could close that gap.

The tool takes into account factors like energy level, available time, budget, and whether a user wants to go out or stay in, then surface personalized suggestions accordingly. For those heading out, it could surface nearby events and activities that match their mood. For a night in, it could help users make the most of what they already have, whether that's streaming, cooking, games, or something else entirely. The result is a visually engaging experience designed to turn low-effort evenings into ones that actually feel worth it.
