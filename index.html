Nightcap

Nightcap is a concept for a digital tool that helps you answer the nightly question: "What should I do tonight?" before you end up three hours deep into your phone wondering where the evening went.

Tell it how you're feeling, how much energy you've got, what your budget looks like, and whether pants are still an option, and it gives you something worth doing. Going out? It finds events and activities that match your vibe. Staying in? It helps you make the most of what you've already got at home. No guilt, no overthinking, just a better evening.
